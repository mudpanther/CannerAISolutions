The Process

How It Works

We handle the full production pipeline. You contribute the expertise — we turn it into a consistent, professional content presence.

01
🎥

You Record Once

Send us a 5–10 minute video of yourself talking naturally — clean background, good lighting, different angles if possible. This is the raw material for your AI clone.

Best results: high-quality camera, natural expressions, full sentences with pauses.
02
🎙️

We Clone Your Voice

Using ElevenLabs, we create a high-fidelity voice clone from your audio. This synthetic voice will narrate every future video with your tone, cadence, and personality.

Professional clone: 30-minute audio sample for maximum realism. Recorded in a quiet environment with a good microphone.
03
🤖

We Build Your Avatar

Using HeyGen, we create a photorealistic AI avatar of you — trained on your video footage. Your avatar lip-syncs perfectly to your cloned voice narration.

Output: Full HD 1920×1080 talking-head video. Typical generation time: 5–60 min depending on length.
04
✍️

You Provide the Script

You (or your team, or AI tools like ChatGPT) write the script. Send it to us as a transcript file. We recommend short sentences, natural pauses, and avoiding overly formal language so the clone sounds like you.

Tip: Use AI to draft scripts in your voice, then review and approve before sending to us.
05
🎬

We Produce the Video

Your cloned voice narrates the script. Your avatar delivers it on screen. We handle the full rendering pipeline — audio sync, avatar generation, and final video export.

Format: 30–60 second clips. Cut every 8–12 seconds. Delivered as full HD MP4 files.
06
📦

We Deliver

You receive polished, ready-to-post MP4 video files on your production schedule. Post them wherever you want — LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook — on your own timeline.

You own the files. You control the posting. We just handle the production.
